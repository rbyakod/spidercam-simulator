## Notes
- Use **identical** motors across all 4 winches
- Buy one spare DRV8825 driver
- Pololu DRV8825 is higher quality than generic clones
- Official Pi camera is more reliable than generic modules
- 2020 V-slot from Openbuilds or RobotDigg ships to both regions
