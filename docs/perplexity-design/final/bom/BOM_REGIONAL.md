## General Sourcing Notes
- Always buy an extra DRV8825 driver
- NEMA 17 motors: at least 40 Ncm holding torque
- Wire rope: 7x7 strand 1.5mm
- Use genuine microSD cards (Samsung Pro Endurance or SanDisk Industrial)
